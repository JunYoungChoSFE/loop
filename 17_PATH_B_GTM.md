# 17 — Path B go-to-market: loyalty for replenishment brands

> Strategic pivot (2026-06-18): stop competing as "another simple loyalty app"
> (a saturated fight against Smile/Joy/Rivo/BON with thousands of reviews).
> Own a defensible niche instead: **loyalty + reorder prediction for consumable /
> replenishment brands** — coffee, supplements, skincare, pet, household refills —
> where repurchase timing is predictable and valuable, and where the generic
> loyalty apps don't compete.
>
> Why this is now real, not just a slogan: the engine uses the merchant's declared
> vertical to set the cold-start reorder prior (commit "Wire shop vertical…"),
> so even a small store with little history gets meaningful day-one predictions —
> exactly the segment where the wedge used to be weakest.

---

## 1. Positioning statement (internal compass)

**For** DTC brands whose customers buy on a cycle (consumables/refills),
**Roost** is a loyalty app that **rewards repeat buying and nudges customers to
reorder right when they're due**, **unlike** generic points apps that ignore
*when* a customer is about to lapse, **and without** the cost and complexity of a
full retention/email platform.

One-line: **"Loyalty that knows when your customers are due to reorder."**

## 3. Cold-outreach plan (first 5–10 installs + reviews)

> Paid "loyalty app" keyword ads with 0 reviews = burning money. At this stage,
> targeted founder-to-founder outreach in the niche has far better ROI.

### Ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Shopify store, **consumable/replenishment** product (coffee, tea, supplements,
  protein, skincare, pet food/treats, cleaning refills, vitamins).
- Small-to-mid DTC: enough repeat customers that reorder timing matters, but **not**
  big enough to run Klaviyo/Stay AI retention ops.
- Bonus signal: subscription-adjacent (customers *could* be on a cycle) but **not**
  forced into a subscription; or frustrated with Smile's order-volume pricing.

### Where to find them
- Shopify-detection tools (BuiltWith / Koala / Storeleads) filtered by category + "uses Shopify".
- Instagram/TikTok DTC consumable brands; their bios link the store.
- Communities: r/shopify, r/ecommerce, indie DTC Slacks/Discords, supplement & coffee
  seller Facebook groups, Indie Hackers.
- Stores that recently churned from a pricey loyalty app (App Store reviews mentioning cost).

### Offer (the hook)
First **10 design-partner stores get Pro free** (e.g., 6–12 months) in exchange for
honest feedback. **No review is required** for the free Pro — asking for a review
only *after* they're genuinely happy, and never gating it, keeps guardrail 2 intact.

### Message template (cold email / DM — keep it short)
```
Subject: reorder reminders for {Brand}?

Hi {Name} — {Brand}'s {coffee/supplements} look like something customers
rebuy on a cycle. I built Roost, a Shopify loyalty app that predicts when
each customer is due to reorder and rewards them for coming back — made
specifically for brands like yours, not a generic points app.

I'm onboarding a few {coffee/supplement} brands as design partners with Pro
free while I learn what actually helps. Worth a 10-min look? Happy to set it
up with you.

— {You}, {store/app link}
```
Personalise the `{vertical}` word every time; never send the generic version.

### Funnel target
~30 well-targeted, personalised touches → 5–10 installs → 5 honest reviews →
*then* the listing reframe (section 2) starts converting organic traffic, and
*then* consider paid acquisition on niche keywords ("supplement loyalty",
"reorder reminder").

### Honest review ask (post-value, never gated)
After a partner has seen real value (points redeemed, a win-back landed), a plain:
*"If Roost's been useful, a short App Store review would genuinely help a new app."*
No incentive tied to it, no feature held hostage.
