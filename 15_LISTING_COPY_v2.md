## App details  (markdown, limit 2800)

```markdown
**Roost is a loyalty app that also tells you which customers are about to stop buying — and helps you keep them.**

Most loyalty apps stop at points. Roost adds a quiet prediction layer: it studies each customer's own purchase rhythm and estimates when they're due to reorder and who's slipping away. You decide what to do with that — nothing happens automatically unless you turn it on.

**Loyalty that runs itself**
- Customers earn points on purchases, signups and referrals at rates you control.
- Points redeem for one-time Shopify discount codes — fixed amount, percentage, or free shipping.
- A storefront widget shows points, rewards and a referral link, matched to your brand colour with no custom CSS.

**Win back customers before they leave (opt-in)**
- Roost flags customers who are drifting away or due to reorder.
- With your consent, it can send a gentle reminder or grant real bonus points to win them back.
- A short digest tells you when a high-value customer is at risk — so you can reach out personally.
- Every nudge carries real value. No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no guilt.

**Built for small merchants**
- Setup takes about five minutes. The dashboard shows your setup status and key numbers at a glance.
- One flat price. The free plan includes the core loyalty features; Pro removes the monthly order cap and unlocks the named churn list and automatic win-backs.
- Uninstall cleanly at any time — Roost removes its widget and your data, leaving no trace in your theme.

Honest points, real rewards, and a heads-up before your customers go quiet.
```
